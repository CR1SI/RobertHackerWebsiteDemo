The Second Renaissance

In The Second Renaissance, Robert Hacker shows how AI has surpassed human cognition, tracing science’s path from the nineteenth century to today. He reveals five pillars to reimagine the future, two paradigms shaping technology and business, and the vast benefits and applications of AI. With practical examples, he offers leaders, researchers, policymakers, and students tools to innovate and adapt in this new era.

The Renaissance Rewired

In The Renaissance Rewired, Robert Hacker shows how AI and modeling are reshaping society, especially government and education. He argues the U.S. federal government must evolve from outdated hierarchy to a decentralized, citizen-focused model using AI for decision-making, transparency, and reduced bureaucracy. With systems thinking, education reform, and cross-sector collaboration, the book offers a roadmap to reimagine democratic governance in the 21st century.

Billion Dollar Company

In Billion Dollar Company, Robert Hacker presents a practical, industry-proven methodology that integrates strategy and finance in a five-step process to develop new business concepts. Illustrated with stories from the author’s success in building a billion-dollar company in Indonesia, it focuses on creating business models through a powerful heuristic and shows how this approach simplifies financial modeling. It also provides an in-depth look at financial models and their role in early-stage capital raising.

Scaling Social Entrepreneurship

In Scaling Social Entrepreneurship, Robert Hacker provides a practical guide to developing scalable social entrepreneurship ventures (SEVs). The author shows how to modify the business model to build large, sustainable organizations addressing social problems in the developing world. Drawing on experience as CFO of One Laptop per Child and building a billion-dollar company in Indonesia, it shares lessons also taught at Florida International University and MIT Sloan.
